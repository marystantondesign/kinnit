Build the foundational architecture for a private, invitation-only relationship platform called Occasions or Celebrations. Do not take design liberties. Do not invent extra features. Do not jump ahead into polished UI. Focus only on clear product architecture, information structure, and basic screen framework. This should feel like a desktop-first app with future mobile and iPad support, but for this prompt, prioritize desktop structure only.

This is not a social media platform. It is a private, consent-based platform where users connect one-to-one or in small trusted networks by invitation only. A person can invite someone by email or phone number, but both people must agree before they become connected. No public profiles, no discovery feed, no follower model, no content posting system.

The purpose of the app is to help people document how they want to be known, celebrated, supported, and cared for, and to help trusted connections reference that information when relevant. This includes preferences, gift guidance, important dates, occasion-related information, and selected personal context. Do not build every future feature yet. Only create the foundation.

- pending invites
- connection status

2. Profile Data

3. Occasions

- celebrations

These should only be represented as categories and objects in the structure, not fully designed workflows yet.

4. Plans
- collaborative planning objects tied to occasions
- these should exist in the architecture, but only at a placeholder structural level for now

5. Privacy and Sharing
- visibility controls
- who can see what
- section-level sharing permissions
