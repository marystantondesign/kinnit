Edit the existing **About Me → Surveys** screen and create a survey called **“Flowers, Plants & Objects.”**

This survey establishes the base pattern for future surveys in the Celebrations app.

Design the survey as a **scrollable card layout** with clearly separated sections. The UI should feel interactive and visual, not like a traditional form.

Use sliders, image selection grids, checkboxes, and optional text inputs.

---

## Survey Intro

Add a short intro block at the top:

**Flowers, Plants & Objects**

“These questions help people know what small gestures you enjoy receiving — things like bouquets, houseplants, cards, sweets, or small tokens.”

---

## Section 1 — Flowers

**Question:** Do you like receiving flowers?

Add a **1–5 slider**

1 — Please don’t give me flowers
2 — Usually no
3 — Occasionally
4 — Yes
5 — I love receiving flowers

If the user selects **1 or 2**, reveal a checkbox list:

Why not?

• I’m allergic to flowers
• I think cut flowers are wasteful
• Pets could get into them
• I prefer plants instead
• They’re hard to maintain
• Other (text field)

---

**Question:** What flower styles do you like?

Create a **multi-select image grid** with photo tiles.

Options:

Classic bouquet
Wildflowers / meadow style
Minimal modern bouquet
Single-flower bouquets
Dramatic unusual flowers
Seasonal local flowers
Hand-picked flowers

---

**Question:** Flower preferences

Add a **tag input and optional text field**

Prompt:
“Favorite flowers, colors, or anything people should know.”

---

## Section 2 — Plants

**Question:** Do you like receiving houseplants?

Add the same **1–5 slider scale**

If the user selects **1 or 2**, reveal reasons:

• Not enough light
• I travel too often
• I already have too many plants
• I struggle to keep them alive
• Pets might eat them
• Other (text field)

## Component Guidance

Use reusable components so this structure can power other surveys:

• slider preference question
• image selection grid
• checkbox question block
• conditional follow-up question
• open reflection input

This survey should serve as the **template structure for all preference surveys in the Celebrations app**.
