Extend the architecture of the prototype by introducing a new object type called Care Reminders.

Do not redesign the interface or remove existing sections. Only introduce the structural concept and basic pages for this new object.

Care Reminders represent personal follow-up actions that help users remember to support or celebrate someone.

Care Reminders are different from Occasions and different from Plans.

Occasions represent meaningful life events.

Plans represent collaborative coordination spaces.

Care Reminders represent personal actions the user wants to remember.


Examples of Care Reminders include:

Send Sarah a birthday card  
Check in with Emma weekly during surgery recovery  
Reach out to Michael on the anniversary of his father's death  
Mail a congratulations card after graduation  
Follow up with a friend after job loss


Add a new section to the main navigation called:

Reminders

This section should display a list of Care Reminders connected to people or occasions.

Each reminder card should show:

the action
the person involved
the date or reminder timing


Examples:

Send birthday card → Sarah Johnson → April 10  
Check in weekly → Emma Davis → During recovery  
Memorial anniversary reminder → Michael Chen → June 5


Reminders may optionally be connected to an Occasion.

Example:
Memorial anniversary reminder → linked to Grandmother Memorial Anniversary.


Care Reminders should be simple personal tasks rather than collaborative workspaces.

They should not replace Plans and they should not replace Occasions.

They exist to help users follow through on caring actions.
